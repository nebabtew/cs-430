# Collection, cleaning, and corroboration

Snapshot accessed October 4, 2026 (America/Chicago). Collector: ChatGPT, acting as an AI assistant for the team. Public provider pages were opened, not inferred from search snippets. Short factual manual observations are preserved unchanged in data/raw/observations.csv. The source register and raw observations preserve the evidence for each classification.

## Scope and counts
The intended user is a student or resident seeking routine automotive maintenance or brake/adjustment services in Columbia, Missouri. Include only a verifiable business identity, a physical Columbia street location, and at least one explicit offering. Keep one selected location per distinct business brand. This is a convenience sample and a bounded offering sample, not an exhaustive local directory.

45 raw observation rows were collected: 15 primary identity/location rows (B01-B15), 20 offering rows (O01-O20), 3 independent-publisher address checks (C01-C03), and 7 review/exclusion rows (E01-E07). Final data: 15 businesses, 4 services, 2 categories, 20 unique business/service pairs, 20 cited pages and 18 distinct publisher names. The three external directory publishers are AAA, Michelin North America, and Better Business Bureau. Distinct provider brand labels are not a claim that all providers have unrelated corporate owners. The independent-publisher minimum is supported by the provider/directory pairs in the corroboration register, regardless of shared corporate ownership among other brands.

## Two rules
R1: Standardize identity/location wording and merge records confirmed to describe the same business. Preserve the original name/address. Use North/N and Street/St consistently where the selected street location is unchanged. Use the current provider-confirmed name after a rebrand. Do not infer identity just from similar names.
R2: Map explicit observed service wording to four controlled service types. Oil/filter/lube headings map to Oil change; brake repair/replacement/service headings map to Brake service; Tire Rotation maps to Tire rotation; Alignments maps to Wheel alignment. Ambiguous tire services alone do not establish tire rotation. Conflicting claims are excluded rather than guessed.

Five actual before/after examples, including the required three, are in data/cleaning_examples.csv. Raw captures remain unchanged. Trim is a routine implementation detail, not a manufactured cleaning example.

## Duplicate handling and exclusions
E01 confirms that Custom Complete Automotive is now Dobbs. E02 confirms Mutrux Auto is now Norris. Neither old alias is counted as an additional business. E05 repeats the same All-Star selected location in its footer, so one Business row remains. E06 (All-Star East) and E07 (A to Z Fay Street) are additional branches; they do not add businesses. The raw records represent 17 encountered location records for the 15 retained brands, of which two branches are excluded.
E03/E04 preserve Tom's conflicting alignment claims. A generic service list mentions Wheel Alignments, but the specific FAQ says it does not offer wheel alignment services. No alignment pair is loaded for Tom. The affirmative oil and brake claims are retained. The 20 retained pairs were deliberately bounded; other affirmative services on the pages were outside this collected offering sample and are not counted.

C03 checks Walt's 1204 N College Ave address against its own service page and BBB. Each external publisher is organizationally separate from the provider, with no explicit shared-upstream citation identified in the opened record. Values, raw IDs, and both exact URLs appear in data/corroboration.csv. Phone numbers/hours were not modeled, so differing directory values for those fields were not treated as verified project facts.

## Evidence and reuse
Source IDs resolve to data/clean/sources.csv and MySQL Source. All sources locate saved manual observations in data/raw/observations.csv. Original service labels are observations; the controlled name and category are our database classifications. A Source FK validates the parent record, while tools/make_load.py validates raw ID/source/URL consistency. The captured wording is manually checked against the opened pages.
Provider pages sometimes describe the brand or multiple locations, especially Dobbs's general service page, Automotive Specialist's services page, A to Z, GAPS and Big O's caveat. Interpret these as business offerings; branch availability unverified. Contact a provider before relying on an appointment or current service. All 15 retained businesses have a verified website, so Q6 returns no rows for the real sample. Synthetic missing websites appear only in T4's isolated database.
Published rights/attribution notes are recorded in data/source_reuse_notes.csv. Full copyrighted pages, logos, reviews, prices and images are not copied into this package. Source facts reflect the accessible pages at collection, and no live website is needed for rebuilding the database.
